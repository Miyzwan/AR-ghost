AR Ghost Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

AR Ghost does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Camera

The camera is used only while the ghost is on screen: to show the ghost in augmented reality and to detect your face and hand gestures. All camera images are processed on your device in real time. They are never saved, and never sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

Photos you take in the app stay in memory until you choose to share or save them. The app only asks for permission to add photos to your library, and only when you save one.

No tracking

AR Ghost has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your settings (such as sound on/off) are stored only on your device.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Kebijakan Privasi AR Ghost

AR Ghost tidak mengumpulkan, menyimpan, atau membagikan data pribadi apa pun.

Kamera hanya dipakai selama hantu tampil di layar, untuk menampilkan hantu dalam augmented reality dan mendeteksi wajah serta gestur tanganmu. Semua gambar kamera diproses langsung di perangkatmu, tidak pernah disimpan, dan tidak pernah dikirim ke mana pun.

Foto yang kamu ambil hanya ada di memori sampai kamu sendiri membagikan atau menyimpannya.

Tidak ada akun, analitik, iklan, maupun pelacakan. Pengaturan (misalnya suara nyala/mati) hanya disimpan di perangkatmu.
